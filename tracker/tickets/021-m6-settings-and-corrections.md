## Question

Build milestone M6: the settings and deletion screens per the privacy model,
`docs/privacy.md` as the single source of that copy, the corrections flow
that feeds the eval dataset, empty states, and the hi-fi sweep.

## Resolution

- **One source for privacy copy**: `packages/domain/src/privacy.ts` holds the
  store / never-store lists, the scope explanation and the four actions with
  their consequences. The permission screen, the new `/privacy` page and
  `docs/privacy.md` (generated by `pnpm docs:privacy`) all render from it.
  The wireframe requires the consent screen and privacy page to match
  word-for-word; this makes drift impossible rather than remembered.
- **Settings** states what is held — mailbox, scope, flights, trips,
  source-email records — then offers the four actions separately, each with
  its consequence spelled out and typed confirmation on the destructive ones.
  Verified the gate blocks until the words are typed; no data was deleted in
  testing.
- **Deletion semantics** as decided: disconnect revokes at Google *and*
  destroys the token but deletes nothing; delete-my-emails removes source
  metadata and extractions while flights survive with provenance showing the
  source deleted; delete-my-history removes derived data but keeps the emails
  so a re-import rebuilds without re-reading Gmail; account deletion removes
  everything including the eval labels.
- **Corrections** work: "Correct this flight" (airport fix) and "Not a
  flight" write immutable `corrections` rows through a definer RPC that also
  applies the effect. This is the feedback loop the known extraction bugs
  need — the `VIL`-for-`VNO` flight can now be corrected by hand, and that
  correction is a labelled example.
- **Empty states** on the overview and trips say what to do next, per year
  filter or overall.

## What M6 leaves for later

- The corrections are recorded but not yet *replayed* over a re-import: the
  pipeline rebuilds flights from extractions and would discard a manual
  airport fix on the next run. Replay is the natural next slice.
- The golden-corpus eval harness (brief §29's ~100 fixtures) is specified but
  not built; corrections now accumulate the real-world half of it.
