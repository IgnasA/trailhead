1MVP flow wireframes — first-run path, greybox, annotated
Assumptions I made
Structure, not style
Greybox blocks; real copy only where the wording is the design (headline, permission language, import log). The Modernist grid, 2px rules and flush-left alignment are already load-bearing here, so the hi-fi pass is a skin, not a re-layout.
Invented data, consistent everywhere
One fictional user: 2019→2026, 132 flights, 47 countries, 91 airports, 312,482 km, 14 airlines. Same figures across every frame so the numbers read as one story.
The magic moment is the product
Built as a scroll story in three stops: one number, then the stack, then the map. The dashboard is where you live afterwards; the reveal is what makes you tell someone.
